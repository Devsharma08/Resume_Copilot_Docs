If your real objective is Anthropic research credits

I would not write a generic white paper titled "AI Resume Screening Using LLMs." There are already many papers covering that broad idea.

A stronger direction would be something narrowly focused and experimentally rigorous, such as:

"Hallucination, Calibration, and Explainability in LLM-Based Resume Screening: A Comparative Evaluation of Proprietary and Open-Source Models."

That title immediately signals:

a measurable research question,
comparison across multiple models,
relevance to Responsible AI,
and practical impact on hiring systems.

It also naturally ties into topics that Anthropic, OpenAI, and the broader AI research community actively care about: reliability, transparency, and trustworthy deployment of LLMs.
